# Edexcel IAL Arsenal: past-paper images

Question and mark-scheme crops for [Edexcel IAL Arsenal](https://github.com/TheCodeHeist/edexcel-ial-arsenal),
a free, non-commercial study site made by students, for students. They are served from GitHub Pages and
loaded by the site. Nothing here is edited by hand: the files are generated by the site repo's `pipeline/`.

```
<subject>/<paper id>/<label>.webp        e.g. physics/WPH14_2026_06/q13_b_i.webp
                     <label>_ms.webp     its mark-scheme rows
```

## Whose work this is

- **The papers and mark schemes are © Pearson Education Ltd** (Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Level).
  They are reproduced here, cropped, for non-commercial study only. Pearson owns them. We do not.
- The files were collected from the free archives that students around the world rely on:
  **[PaperLords](https://www.paperlords.org/ial)**, whose team collects them and keeps them up to date for
  students everywhere, and **[Physics & Maths Tutor](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/)**. Every paper on
  the site names the archive its files came from and links back to the original.

If you are a rights holder and want something removed, please open an issue and it will be taken down.
